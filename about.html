WHO WE ARE

Rooted here.
Connected by water.

One Earth – One Ocean Philippines brings a locally focused perspective to a shared mission: healthier ecosystems and a future with less pollution.

Why the
Philippines?

Our islands, waterways, and coastlines connect biodiversity, livelihoods, culture, and everyday life.

Meaningful environmental action requires more than removing waste. It also means listening, learning, building relationships, and investing in solutions that communities can carry forward.

We are part of the wider One Earth – One Ocean network, with a dedicated focus on Philippine projects and partnerships.
